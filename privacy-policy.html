Alba Business Desk is the name of the software; Alba Designs is the business that owns and operates it, and is the party legally responsible for the information described here. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you use Alba Business Desk, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA").

1a. Workspaces and organisations

Your data is held in a workspace (an "organisation") rather than being tied to your personal login alone. This is what allows a business to have more than one person working in the same CRM in future. Access to a workspace is controlled by a membership record with a role — owner, admin, member, or viewer — and is enforced at the database level, not merely hidden in the interface.

If a member is removed from a workspace, their access ends immediately. We keep a record that they were a member and when they were removed, so the workspace owner has an accurate history of who had access — we do not silently erase that.

1. Who we are, and in what capacity

We act as the responsible party for the account and billing information described below, and as an operator for the business data — contacts, companies, deals, tasks and notes — that you or your organisation enter into the CRM about your own customers. You remain the responsible party for that data.

2. What information we collect

Account information

You sign in with Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication. We receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account. We never receive or store your Google password.

CRM data you enter

Everything you type into the CRM — contacts, companies, deals, tasks, notes, and custom fields — is stored so the tool can work. This may include personal information about your own customers, which you control and are responsible for entering lawfully.

Billing and payment information

Subscriptions are billed at a flat rate through PayFast, a South African payment gateway. We never see or store your card number — PayFast collects payment details directly on its own secure payment page. What we store is your subscription status, the payment amount, the payment date and time, and the PayFast-issued payment reference, so we can show you a statement and confirm your account is active.

Connected social media and calendar accounts (optional)

If you connect Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok accounts for the Social tab, we store the access tokens needed to fetch your follower counts and post engagement, along with the data those platforms return — follower counts, post captions, likes, comments, and shares. If you connect Google Calendar, we store the access needed to check your availability and create, update, or delete calendar events on your behalf. We request only the minimum access needed to provide each feature, and each connection belongs to your account individually — it is never shared with anyone else using the platform.

Workspace membership records

For each workspace you belong to we store your user ID, email address, display name, role, and whether your membership is active — this is what determines what you are permitted to see and do.

Technical and security information

We log IP addresses and timestamps for security purposes: to detect abuse, enforce rate limits, and maintain an audit trail of account and billing events such as sign-ins tied to payment actions, subscription changes, and account deletions.

Some providers (Google Firebase/Cloud, Vercel) operate infrastructure outside South Africa, so personal information may be processed or stored on servers in other countries as a normal part of how those services work. Where this happens we rely on those providers' own data protection commitments and standard contractual safeguards, consistent with the cross-border transfer conditions in section 72 of POPIA.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your account and CRM data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account through the Billing tab, we:

  1. Cancel any active subscription first, so you're never charged again;
  2. Delete your CRM data, payment history, and sign-in record;
  3. Delete your connected social media and calendar credentials;
  4. Delete your workspace and every record in it — contacts, companies, deals, tasks, activity and settings;
  5. Retain a limited security audit log entry — that an account existed and was deleted, with a timestamp — for a reasonable period afterward, for fraud prevention and legal compliance.

Separately from full account deletion, disconnecting a single integration deletes that integration's stored credentials immediately, while leaving the rest of your workspace untouched.

7a. How your workspace data is stored

Your workspace data is stored as individual records — each contact, company, deal, task and activity is held separately, with your workspace settings kept alongside them. Access to every one of those records is controlled by your workspace membership and role.

8. How we protect information

Access to your data is restricted at the database level so only active members of your workspace can read or write it, enforced by Firestore security rules rather than application logic alone. Your role within the workspace determines what you may do, and that check happens in the database itself — a read-only member cannot write data even if the interface were bypassed. Paid features additionally verify the workspace's subscription at the database level, not merely in the interface. Records are validated on write for size and shape, and your activity history is append-only: it can be added to, but not edited or deleted by any client, so the record of what happened in your workspace stays trustworthy. For performance, only recent activity is loaded when you sign in; the rest is retrieved on demand when you open the relevant record or ask for older entries — nothing is deleted, it is simply not fetched until needed. Billing and subscription records can only ever be written by our verified backend, never directly by a user. Payment webhook notifications are cryptographically verified before being trusted. We use HTTPS everywhere, and sensitive session cookies are marked HttpOnly and Secure so they cannot be read by page scripts or sent over an unencrypted connection.

9. Your rights under POPIA

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information
  • Request deletion of your personal information — you can do this yourself at any time via Billing → Delete my account & all data
  • Object to our processing on reasonable grounds
  • Complain to South Africa's Information Regulator if you believe your information has been mishandled

You can also withdraw consent for any connected integration at any time, without contacting us: the Settings → Connected accounts panel disconnects Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, and the Calendar tab disconnects Google Calendar. Where the platform supports it we ask them to revoke the access token as well, and the stored credentials are deleted from our side either way. Disconnecting stops future syncing; data already pulled into your workspace stays until you delete it.
