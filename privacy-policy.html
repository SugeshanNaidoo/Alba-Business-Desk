This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you use Alba Business Desk, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA").

1. Who we are, and in what capacity

We act as the responsible party for the account and billing information described below, and as an operator for the business data — contacts, companies, deals, tasks, notes, and WhatsApp message content — that you or your organisation enter into the CRM about your own customers. You remain the responsible party for that data.

2. What information we collect

Account information

You sign in with Google Sign-In via Firebase Authentication. We receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account. We never receive or store your Google password.

CRM data you enter

Everything you type into the CRM — contacts, companies, deals, tasks, notes, and custom fields — is stored so the tool can work. This may include personal information about your own customers, which you control and are responsible for entering lawfully.

Billing and payment information

Subscriptions are billed at a flat rate through PayFast, a South African payment gateway. We never see or store your card number — PayFast collects payment details directly on its own secure payment page. What we store is your subscription status, the payment amount, the payment date and time, and the PayFast-issued payment reference, so we can show you a statement and confirm your account is active.

Connected social media and calendar accounts (optional)

If you connect Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok accounts for the Social tab, we store the access tokens needed to fetch your follower counts and post engagement, along with the data those platforms return — follower counts, post captions, likes, comments, and shares. If you connect Google Calendar, we store the access needed to check your availability and create, update, or delete calendar events on your behalf. We request only the minimum access needed to provide each feature, and each connection belongs to your account individually — it is never shared with anyone else using the platform.

WhatsApp messages (optional)

If you connect a WhatsApp Business number, we store the credentials needed to send and receive messages on your behalf, and we store the content of messages exchanged with your contacts — both what they send you and what you send them — so the conversation history is available in the contact's record. This is genuinely more sensitive than follower counts or post engagement, since it's real conversation content, and it's treated accordingly: message history is kept in its own restricted store, never exposed through any public or unauthenticated channel, and only ever readable by your own signed-in account.

Technical and security information

We log IP addresses and timestamps for security purposes: to detect abuse, enforce rate limits, and maintain an audit trail of account and billing events such as sign-ins tied to payment actions, subscription changes, and account deletions.

We use a small number of cookies and no advertising or tracking cookies. The full list, with durations, is in the Cookie Policy.

5. Who we share information with

We share information with these service providers, only as needed to run Alba Business Desk:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — authentication, database hosting (Firestore), and Google Calendar access where you've connected it
  • Vercel — hosting for the application and backend functions
  • PayFast — payment processing for subscriptions
  • Meta (Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp Business Platform) and TikTok — only if you connect those accounts, to retrieve your own social media statistics or send and receive WhatsApp messages

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

6. Cross-border storage and transfers

Some providers (Google Firebase/Cloud, Vercel) operate infrastructure outside South Africa, so personal information may be processed or stored on servers in other countries as a normal part of how those services work. Where this happens we rely on those providers' own data protection commitments and standard contractual safeguards, consistent with the cross-border transfer conditions in section 72 of POPIA.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your account and CRM data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account through the Billing tab, we:

  1. Cancel any active subscription first, so you're never charged again;
  2. Delete your CRM data, payment history, and sign-in record;
  3. Delete your connected social media, calendar, and WhatsApp credentials, and all stored WhatsApp message history;
  4. Retain a limited security audit log entry — that an account existed and was deleted, with a timestamp — for a reasonable period afterward, for fraud prevention and legal compliance.

8. How we protect information

Access to your data is restricted at the database level so only your own signed-in account can read or write it, enforced by Firestore security rules rather than application logic alone. Billing and subscription records can only ever be written by our verified backend, never directly by a user. Payment and WhatsApp webhook notifications are cryptographically verified before being trusted. We use HTTPS everywhere, and sensitive session cookies are marked HttpOnly and Secure so they cannot be read by page scripts or sent over an unencrypted connection.

  • Request deletion of your personal information — you can do this yourself at any time via Billing → Delete my account & all data
