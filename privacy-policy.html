1. Who we are

Alba Business Desk is a CRM (customer relationship management) tool — a product of Alba Designs ("we", "us", "our"), registered as [insert Alba Designs' registration number], based in South Africa. Alba Business Desk is the name of the software; Alba Designs is the business that owns and operates it, and is the party legally responsible for the information described in this policy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you use Alba Business Desk, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 ("POPIA").

We act as the responsible party for the account and billing information described below, and as an operator processing the business data (contacts, deals, notes, etc.) that you or your organisation enters into the CRM on your customers' behalf — you remain the responsible party for that data.

2. What information we collect

Account information

When you sign in, we use Google Sign-In (via Firebase Authentication) to authenticate you. We receive your name, email address, and profile photo from your Google account. We do not receive or store your Google password.

CRM data you enter

Everything you type into the CRM — contacts, companies, deals, tasks, notes, custom fields, and similar — is stored so the tool can work. This may include personal information about your own customers or contacts, which you control and are responsible for entering lawfully.

Billing and payment information

Subscriptions are billed at a flat rate through PayFast, a South African payment gateway. We never see or store your card number — PayFast collects payment details directly on their own secure payment page. What we do store is: the subscription status, payment amount, payment date and time, and a PayFast-issued payment reference number, so we can show you a statement and confirm your account is active.

Connected social media accounts (optional)

If you choose to connect Instagram, Facebook, or TikTok accounts for the Social tab, we store the access tokens needed to fetch your follower counts and post engagement, along with the data those platforms return (follower counts, post captions, likes, comments, shares). We only request the minimum access needed to display this information back to you.

Technical and security information

We log IP addresses and timestamps for security purposes — to detect abuse, enforce rate limits, and maintain an audit trail of account and billing events (sign-ins tied to payment actions, subscription changes, account deletions). We also set a small number of cookies, described below.

5. Who we share information with

We share information with the following service providers, only as needed to run Alba Business Desk:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — authentication and database hosting (Firestore)
  • Vercel — hosting for the application and backend functions
  • PayFast — payment processing for subscriptions
  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and TikTok — only if you choose to connect those accounts, to retrieve your own social media statistics

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

6. Cross-border storage and transfers

Some of our service providers (Google Firebase/Cloud, Vercel) operate infrastructure outside South Africa, which means personal information may be processed or stored on servers in other countries as a normal part of how these services work. Where this happens, we rely on these providers' own data protection commitments and standard contractual safeguards, consistent with the cross-border transfer conditions set out in Section 72 of POPIA.

7. How long we keep information

We keep your account and CRM data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account through the Billing tab, we:

  1. Cancel any active subscription first (so you're never charged again),
  2. Delete your CRM data, payment history, and sign-in record,
  3. Retain a limited security audit log entry (that an account existed and was deleted, with a timestamp) for a reasonable period afterward, for fraud prevention and legal compliance purposes.

8. How we protect information

Access to your data is restricted at the database level so that only your own signed-in account can read or write it — this is enforced by Firestore security rules, not just application logic. Billing and subscription records can only ever be written by our verified backend, never directly by any user. Payment webhook notifications are cryptographically verified before being trusted. We use HTTPS everywhere, and sensitive session cookies are marked HttpOnly and Secure so they can't be read by page scripts or sent over an unencrypted connection.

9. Your rights under POPIA

As a data subject, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information
  • Request deletion of your personal information (you can do this yourself at any time via Billing → Delete my account & all data)
  • Object to our processing of your information on reasonable grounds
  • Complain to South Africa's Information Regulator if you believe your information has been mishandled

To exercise any of these rights beyond what you can already do in the app, contact our Information Officer using the details below.
