Refund Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026 · Applies to the Alba Business Desk CRM tool

This policy explains when Alba Business Desk (the "Service") will refund money you've paid us, and when we won't. We've kept it short and specific on purpose. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

1. Exploring before you subscribe is free

You can sign in and look around the whole CRM without paying anything — but you can't save changes, connect integrations, or send messages until you subscribe. Because nothing is charged during that stage, refunds don't come up. If you've already subscribed and want to stop, see section 3.

2. How billing works

Alba Business Desk is a single flat plan: R699 per month per business, not per user, billed monthly in advance through PayFast. If you subscribe on the 10th, you're paying for service through the 10th of next month, and your payment method is automatically charged again on that date unless you cancel first. Full details are in our Terms of Service.

3. 7-day satisfaction guarantee on your first payment

If it's your first time subscribing to Alba Business Desk and you change your mind within 7 days of that first charge, we'll refund you in full — no questions asked.

To claim the guarantee:

  • Email albawagdesigns@gmail.com within 7 days of the first charge.
  • Include the email address on your Alba Business Desk account and the date of the charge.
  • We'll confirm, refund to the original payment method, and cancel the subscription so you aren't charged again.

Refunds usually clear within 3–5 business days, depending on your bank and PayFast. Your workspace data isn't deleted when a refund is issued — you keep access to export it, and it's only removed if you delete your account yourself.

4. After the 7-day window — no pro-rata refunds

After the first 7 days, or on any renewal charge after your first paid month, we don't issue pro-rata refunds for unused time in a billing period. What happens instead:

  • You cancel any time from the Billing tab in the CRM.
  • Your subscription stops renewing — no further charges. We confirm the cancellation directly with PayFast before marking it cancelled, so "cancelled" genuinely means no more charges.
  • You keep full access until the end of the period you've already paid for, after which the workspace becomes read-only for every member. Your data stays intact and exportable — nothing is deleted when a subscription lapses.

This is standard practice for month-to-month software — the fair trade for monthly billing with no long-term contract or setup fee.

5. Billing errors and duplicate charges

If you've been charged incorrectly, charged twice by mistake, or charged after a successful cancellation, we'll refund the incorrect amount in full. Email albawagdesigns@gmail.com and we'll investigate and correct it — always, regardless of how long ago it happened.

6. Service outages

If Alba Business Desk is materially unavailable for an extended period and that causes you real harm, contact us and we'll consider a goodwill credit or partial refund case by case. We don't operate under a contractual service level agreement, so this isn't a formal entitlement — but we want customers treated fairly when something breaks on our side. Note that the Service depends on third parties (Google Firebase, Vercel, PayFast, Meta) whose outages are outside our control; see our Disclaimer.

7. Chargebacks

Please email us before filing a chargeback with your bank. Almost every billing concern can be resolved in a single email; chargebacks cost both sides fees and close the door on a direct refund. If a chargeback is filed in bad faith — without contacting us, or for a charge that was clearly legitimate — we may suspend the account until the disputed amount is settled.

8. How to request a refund

One channel for all refund requests: albawagdesigns@gmail.com. Tell us:

  • The email address on your account.
  • The date and amount of the charge.
  • Whether you're claiming the 7-day guarantee, reporting a billing error, or asking for a goodwill refund.

We'll respond within 2 business days.

9. Governing law

Refunds are subject to the law of the Republic of South Africa, and nothing in this policy limits any rights you have under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 that cannot be excluded by agreement.
