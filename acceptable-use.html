Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026 · Applies to the Alba Business Desk CRM tool and its website

This policy sets out what may and may not be done with Alba Business Desk. It forms part of the Terms of Service.

1. You agree not to

  • Break any applicable law, including data protection law regarding the information you enter about your own contacts.
  • Attempt to gain unauthorized access to another account, another organisation's workspace, or to the underlying systems.
  • Attempt to circumvent workspace roles or permissions, or to grant yourself access or entitlements you have not been given.
  • Abuse, overload, or attempt to circumvent rate limits or security controls on the service.
  • Use the service, including the WhatsApp integration, to send unsolicited bulk communications.
  • Use the WhatsApp integration in a way that violates Meta's WhatsApp Business Policy — including messaging people who haven't opted in to hear from you.
  • Use the Google Calendar integration to add events or invite attendees who have not agreed to hear from you, or in any way that breaches Google's own terms.
  • Upload malware, or probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the platform without written permission.
  • Reverse engineer, resell, sublicense, or white-label the platform without written agreement.
  • Share sign-in credentials outside your business, or use one subscription to operate workspaces for unrelated businesses.
  • Store content that is unlawful, defamatory, or infringes another party's intellectual property.

2. Data you enter about other people

You are the responsible party for personal information you capture about your own customers and contacts — including any WhatsApp conversation history. You must have a lawful basis for holding it, and you must respond to those people's POPIA requests yourself — we process that data on your instruction as an operator.

3. Fair use

The subscription covers your business and its staff. Automated or programmatic access must stay within published rate limits and may not be used to bulk-extract the platform's functionality.

4. Enforcement

We investigate suspected breaches using the security audit log. Depending on severity we may contact you, suspend affected functionality, or terminate the account. Serious or unlawful conduct is reported to the relevant authorities.

5. Reporting abuse

Report suspected abuse to albawagdesigns@gmail.com.
