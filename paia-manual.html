3. Records held

  • Company records: founding documents, resolutions, statutory registers
  • Subscriber records: account details from Google Sign-In, subscription status, connected-integration credentials (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Google Calendar), support correspondence
  • Billing records: PayFast payment references, amounts, dates, and statements
  • Workspace records: organisation details, membership records (user ID, email, display name, role, status, and removal history where applicable)
  • Platform records: security audit logs of account and billing events, IP addresses, and timestamps
  • Financial and tax records
  • Records held under other legislation, including the Companies Act, Tax Administration Act, ECTA, and POPIA

CRM content captured by a subscriber — their contacts, deals, tasks and notes — is held on that subscriber's behalf as an operator. Requests for those records should be directed to the subscriber concerned.

4. Requesting a record

Complete the prescribed PAIA request form and send it to the Information Officer using the contact details above. State which record you want, the form of access, and how you wish to be notified. Where the record is not your own personal information, identify the right you are seeking to exercise or protect and explain why the record is required for it.

5. Fees and timeframes

Prescribed request and access fees apply, except for a request by a person for their own personal information. We respond within 30 days of a valid request, and will notify you if that period is extended as PAIA allows.

6. Refusal and remedies

Access may be refused on the grounds set out in PAIA, including protection of third-party privacy, commercial information, and legally privileged records. If a request is refused you may complain to the Information Regulator or apply to court.
