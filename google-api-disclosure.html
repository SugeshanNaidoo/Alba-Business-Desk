Google API Disclosure

Last updated: 6 August 2026 · Applies to the Google Calendar integration in the Alba Business Desk CRM tool
Alba Business Desk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This page explains how Alba Business Desk (the "Service") accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google user data when you connect Google Calendar. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

1. What Alba Business Desk is

Alba Business Desk is a customer relationship management tool for South African businesses — contacts, companies, deals, tasks, calendar, and reporting in one workspace. It is a product of Alba Designs (registration number 2020/636646/07). The Google integration is an optional Calendar feature: you connect your own Google account so you can see and manage your calendar from inside the CRM instead of switching between two tools.

2. Google APIs and OAuth scopes we request

When you choose to connect Google Calendar, Alba Business Desk requests these OAuth scopes:

ScopeWhy we request it
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events Read your upcoming events so the Calendar tab can display them, and create, update, or delete events you manage from within the CRM.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email Read the email address of the Google account you connect, purely so the CRM can show you which account is linked.

We request only these two scopes. We deliberately do not request the full calendar scope (which would grant access to calendar settings and calendar management beyond events), and we do not request userinfo.profile. Connecting Google is entirely optional — every other part of Alba Business Desk works without it.

Signing in to Alba Business Desk also uses Google Sign-In, through Firebase Authentication. That is a separate mechanism from this integration: it confirms who you are and gives us your name, email address, and profile photo, but grants no access to your calendar. Connecting Google Calendar is a distinct, deliberate second step.

3. How we use Google user data

We use Google user data solely to provide these user-facing Calendar features:

  • Viewing your calendar — reading events in the date range you're looking at, so the month grid in the Calendar tab shows what's actually scheduled.
  • Creating events — adding an event to your calendar when you create one in the CRM.
  • Updating and deleting events — changing or removing an event when you edit or delete it in the CRM.
  • Google Meet links (optional) — when you tick the Meet option on an event, creating a Meet conference link on that calendar event.
  • Account label — storing and displaying the connected account's email so you can confirm which calendar is linked.

These uses are limited to providing prominent, user-facing features in the CRM's Calendar tab. We do not use Google user data for advertising, credit scoring, personalised ads, or any analytics unrelated to operating this integration. We do not use it to train machine learning or AI models.

4. What Google user data we access and store

  • OAuth refresh token — for the connected account, so the integration keeps working without asking you to sign in repeatedly.
  • Account email — the Google account email returned by userinfo.email.
  • Calendar identifier — which calendar we sync to (your primary calendar).
  • Event fields we write — titles, start and end times, descriptions, attendee email addresses, and Meet conference data for events you create or edit through the CRM.

Event data we read for display is fetched live from Google each time you open the Calendar tab and rendered in your browser — we do not keep a copy of your calendar in our database.

We do not sell Google user data. We do not use it to serve advertisements. We do not allow humans to read it except: with your explicit consent for support; where necessary for security investigations such as abuse; where required by law; or where the data has been aggregated and no longer identifies you.

5. Sharing and transfers

We do not transfer Google user data to third parties except as allowed under the Google API Services User Data Policy Limited Use requirements, for example:

  • To provide the Calendar features described above, including writing events back to Google Calendar and Meet through Google's own APIs.
  • For security purposes, such as investigating abuse or protecting accounts.
  • To comply with applicable law.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, and then only with explicit prior user consent where Google's policy requires it.

Infrastructure providers that host Alba Business Desk — Google Cloud / Firebase for the database, and Vercel for the application and its backend functions — process protected application data as part of operating the Service. They are not granted independent rights to use Google user data for their own purposes.

6. Storage and security

  • OAuth tokens are stored in our production database, in a per-account record that is backend-only: database security rules block all client access to it, so it can never be read from a browser, including by you.
  • Every calendar request requires a valid signed-in session, verified server-side. Session cookies are HttpOnly and Secure, and every state-changing request additionally requires a CSRF token.
  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Each customer's Google connection is isolated to their own account — connections are never shared across accounts on the platform.

7. Retention

  • While connected — the refresh token and calendar link metadata are kept so the integration keeps working.
  • After you disconnect — we ask Google to revoke the token, then delete the stored connection record.
  • If you delete your account — the Google connection is deleted along with the rest of your data, as described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Events already in your Google Calendar stay in your calendar. Disconnecting stops our access; it does not delete your calendar entries.

8. How to connect and disconnect

  1. Sign in to Alba Business Desk and open the Calendar tab.
  2. Choose Connect Google Calendar and complete Google's consent screen.
  3. To disconnect later, the same button on the Calendar tab now reads Disconnect Google Calendar — click it. You can also revoke access at any time directly from your Google Account's permissions page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

9. Limited Use affirmation

Alba Business Desk's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements:

  • We limit use to providing user-facing features that are prominent in our interface.
  • We do not transfer Google user data except as Limited Use allows.
  • We do not use Google user data for serving advertisements.
  • We do not allow humans to read the data except under the narrow exceptions Google permits — your consent, security investigations, legal compliance, or aggregated non-identifying data.
