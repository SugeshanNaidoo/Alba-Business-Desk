Cookie Policy

Last updated: 6 August 2026 · Applies to the Alba Business Desk CRM tool and its website

Alba Business Desk uses a small number of cookies. None of them are used for advertising, and we do not use third-party tracking or analytics cookies.

1. The cookies we set

CookiePurposeDuration
abd_sessionKeeps you signed in to billing and account features. Cannot be read by JavaScript (HttpOnly).Up to 5 days, or until you sign out
abd_csrfConfirms billing and account requests genuinely came from your own browser session, not a forged request from another site.Matches the session cookie
pf_oauth_state_metaPrevents forged connection attempts while linking a Facebook account.10 minutes, deleted after use
pf_oauth_state_instagramPrevents forged connection attempts while linking an Instagram account.10 minutes, deleted after use
pf_oauth_state_tiktokPrevents forged connection attempts while linking a TikTok account.10 minutes, deleted after use
gcal_oauth_statePrevents forged connection attempts while linking Google Calendar.10 minutes, deleted after use

All of these are marked HttpOnly and Secure, so they cannot be read by page scripts or sent over an unencrypted connection.

2. Local storage

We also use your browser's local storage — not a cookie, but similar in effect — for a few things:

  • Remembering your light/dark theme preference
  • If you're not signed in, keeping your CRM data cached on your own device
  • Remembering that you've seen and accepted this site's cookie notice

None of this data leaves your browser unless you sign in and enable cloud sync.

3. Third-party cookies

Connecting a social media account involves Meta or TikTok, connecting a calendar involves Google, and payment involves PayFast; those services may set their own cookies on their own pages, governed by their own policies. We set no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies of our own.

4. Managing cookies

Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies in its privacy settings. Blocking our session cookies will prevent you from signing in and using billing or account features. Clearing local storage will reset your theme preference and, if you're working offline, remove locally stored CRM data from that device.
